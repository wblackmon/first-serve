Ministry Presentation

The Cause

The Cause is the work God has entrusted to us, what we do, why we do it, and who we reach.

Through our ministry, we:

  • Teach tennis to children while sharing the truth of the Gospel and the love of Jesus.
  • Witness to anyone the Holy Spirit brings across our path, especially the homeless.
  • Distribute God's Word and encourage people to come to church.
  • Pray with anyone willing to receive prayer.
  • Visit churches to share our ministry and invite partnership and support.

"The harvest is plentiful, but the laborers are few; therefore, pray earnestly to the Lord of the harvest to send out laborers into his harvest."

Matthew 9:37-38

Testimonial

Our ministry reaches people from many backgrounds and cultures. For example, we are currently witnessing to a Chinese family and their daughter. She showes tremendous potential and has greatly advanced in just 1 year.

God continues to bless nearly everything we do in this tennis and witnessing ministry. The reception has been overwhelmingly positive.

The Call

The Call is how God moved my heart from concern to action, and how He equipped me for this work.

God has given me a deep love for people and a burden for the lost. He has equipped me to share Scripture, evangelize, and serve wherever He leads.

"How then will they call on him in whom they have not believed? And how are they to believe in him of whom they have never heard? And how are they to hear without someone preaching?"

"How beautiful are the feet of those who preach the good news!"

Romans 10:14-15

Regarding Tennis

I grew up on the tennis court. I played junior tennis in Northern California and was ranked number 27. I played high school tennis and one year at Auburn University. My father was a Christian and an NCAA tennis coach at multiple universities. Because of him, I learned how to teach and coach tennis at any level, from beginner to world-class.

I have never charged for tennis lessons. One family brings food to help us, but we operate almost entirely by faith.

The Challenge

The Challenge is the invitation, calling others to join in the work God is doing.

We want people to feel personally connected to this ministry. They can:

  • Join us on mission,
  • Pray for us,
  • Or give financially to support the work.

We need minimal tennis equipment: racquets, balls, a basket, gas for transportation, and a small amount of food. Any help with basic bills is deeply appreciated. Their support allows them to make a difference beyond what their church tithe already accomplishes.

From here, we invite people into further conversation. We want this to be more than a presentation. We want partnership. We ask if they can meet with us this week to talk more about joining the work God is doing.
